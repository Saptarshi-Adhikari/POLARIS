# 🧭 Route Optimization & Telemetry Metrics

This document outlines the math cost functions, A* heuristic grids, and validation structures governing the ASTRALIS routing optimization pipeline.

---

## 1. NAVIGATION COST GRID EQUATION

For every cell $(r, c)$ in the A* search grid, the total traversal cost is defined as:
$$\text{totalCost} = \text{distanceCost} + \text{icebergRiskCost} + \text{seaIceCost} + \text{currentResistanceCost} + \text{uncertaintyCost} + \text{boundaryCost}$$

Each cost is normalized:
- **distanceCost**: Euclidean step length ($1.0$ for orthogonal, $1.414$ for diagonal moves).
- **icebergRisk**: Derived from Gaussian hazard decay zones generated by the `RiskIntelligenceEngine`.
- **seaIceCost**: Proportionate to the local sea-ice concentration factor.
- **currentResistance**: Lateral current vector resistance component opposing the forward thrust velocity.

---

## 2. A* PATHFINDING & HAZARD BUFFERING

1. **Heuristic**: Employs an admissible Euclidean distance heuristic to guarantee optimal routes.
2. **Hazard Buffering**: Collision radii of icebergs are padded with safety buffers. High-uncertainty predictions from the `ConfidenceIntelligenceEngine` expand the avoidance buffers dynamically.

---

## 3. ROUTE VALIDATION PIPELINE

Before any recalculated route is accepted, it is validated via `validateRoute(route)` which checks:
- **Obstacle Collisions**: Ensures no segment crosses the critical hazard zone of any iceberg.
- **Self-Intersections**: Prevents route loops or trajectory oscillations.
- **Length Overhead**: Rejects routes with length exceeding $2.8\times$ the direct straight-line distance.
