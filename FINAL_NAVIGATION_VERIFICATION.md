# FINAL NAVIGATION & SAFETY INTEGRATION VERIFICATION

## 1. Executive Summary & Verification Mandate

The **POLARIS Deterministic Navigation & Safety Engine** has undergone comprehensive audit, upgrade, and end-to-end verification.

- **Zero ML Steering/Avoidance**: ML models remain strictly confined to non-authoritative prediction (e.g. drift forecasting, optional risk score hints). All steering, hazard avoidance, route planning, and emergency maneuvers are **100% deterministic and explainable**.
- **Shared DEMO / REAL Safety Stack**: Both DEMO and REAL modes execute the **exact same deterministic safety architecture**:
  $$\text{DATA SOURCE} \rightarrow \text{NORMALIZED ENVIRONMENT STATE} \rightarrow \text{HAZARD MODEL} \rightarrow \text{PREDICTED HAZARD OCCUPANCY} \rightarrow \text{HARD SAFETY CONSTRAINTS} \rightarrow \text{A* + LOS SHORTCUTTING} \rightarrow \text{DUBINS-STYLE SMOOTHING} \rightarrow \text{LOS GUIDANCE} \rightarrow \text{SHIP DYNAMICS} \rightarrow \text{SAFETY GUARD} \rightarrow \text{TELEMETRY/RADAR}$$
- **Non-Traversable Hard Hazard Envelopes**: Predicted hazard footprint cones ($0\text{h}, 6\text{h}, 12\text{h}, 24\text{h}$ OpenDrift-style uncertainty growth) + vessel maneuvering safety margins ($80\text{ SU}$) are rasterized into the cost grid as **strictly non-traversable (`Infinity` / hard blocked)** cells.
- **Single Active Route**: A single canonical `activeRoute` object is generated by the worker planner, adopted by `state.navigation.activeRoute`, followed by `Ship.js` LOS guidance, and rendered by `CanvasRenderer.js`.
- **Planner Algorithm Accuracy**: POLARIS retains its existing grid A* planner and applies post-search line-of-sight shortcutting to reduce unnecessary waypoint vertices and create an any-angle route.
